DarkOps

A private, single-operator tool for organizing and publishing videos to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Reads finished videos and their metadata (title, description, tags, schedule) from the operator's local folders.
  • Shows a daily publishing queue and keeps track of what was published, and where.
  • With the operator's authorization, uploads videos and metadata to the operator's own accounts through the official APIs (YouTube Data API, Instagram API, TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Reads basic channel and video statistics from those same accounts, so the operator can follow performance.

Who it is for

DarkOps is not a public service. There is no sign-up: it runs on the operator's own computer and is used only with accounts that belong to the operator.
