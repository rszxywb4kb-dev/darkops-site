Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

DarkOps ("the app") is a private tool run by a single operator on their own computer to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

Only after the operator signs in and grants permission with Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) or TikTok, the app may access:

  • Account identifiers: channel or account ID, handle and display name, to know which account a video goes to.
  • Publishing access: permission to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and to set their privacy and publish time.
  • Statistics: aggregate channel and video metrics such as views, likes, comments, subscribers/followers and watch time.
  • Access tokens issued by those services, so the app can act on the operator's behalf.

The app does not access viewers' personal data, private messages, contacts or payment information.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the operator's own videos, to record what was published and its public link, and to show statistics to the operator. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not sold.

Storage and security

All data stays on the operator's own computer: records in a local database and access tokens in a local file excluded from version control. The app has no server of its own and does not send this data to third parties, other than the request itself to the service being used (Google, Meta or TikTok).

Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone. Videos and metadata are sent only to the platform the operator chose to publish on.

Cookies

This website and the app do not use cookies or tracking.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google/YouTube: remove "DarkOps" at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google security settings).
  • Instagram: remove "DarkOps" under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites in Instagram.
  • TikTok: remove "DarkOps" under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

After access is revoked the stored tokens stop working. Stored statistics and records are deleted by the operator from the local database on request, within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/rszxywb4kb-dev/darkops-site/issues.

Política de Privacidade

Atualizada em 4 de novembro de 2026

O DarkOps é uma ferramenta privada, usada por um único operador no próprio computador, para publicar vídeos nas contas próprias dele no YouTube, Instagram e TikTok. Não é aberta ao público e não tem cadastro.

O que acessa: só depois de o operador entrar e autorizar com o Google (YouTube), a Meta (Instagram) ou o TikTok, o app acessa a identificação da conta, a permissão para enviar vídeos com título, descrição, tags e capa, métricas agregadas (views, likes, comentários, inscritos/seguidores, tempo de exibição) e os tokens de acesso. Não acessa dados pessoais de espectadores, mensagens, contatos nem pagamentos.

Uso: só para enviar e agendar os vídeos do operador, registrar o que foi publicado e mostrar as métricas a ele. Nada é vendido nem usado para publicidade.

Armazenamento: tudo fica no computador do operador (banco local e arquivo de tokens fora do controle de versão). Não há servidor próprio nem compartilhamento com terceiros além da própria plataforma de publicação. Sem cookies.

Google e YouTube: o app usa os YouTube API Services e segue os Termos de Serviço do YouTube, a Política de Privacidade do Google e a Política de Dados do Usuário dos Serviços de API do Google, incluindo os requisitos de Uso Limitado.

Revogar e excluir: remova o "DarkOps" em myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), em Configurações → Permissões de sites → Apps e sites (Instagram) ou em Configurações e privacidade → Segurança → Permissões de apps (TikTok). Os registros locais são excluídos a pedido, em até 30 dias.

Contato: abra uma issue em github.com/rszxywb4kb-dev/darkops-site/issues.
